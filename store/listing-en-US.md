## Full description

WordBloom is a relaxing word-connect game with ten language editions. Swipe between letter tiles to discover words, complete connected crossword grids and grow an illustrated garden. Play the included puzzles offline, without ads or a WordBloom account.

WORD PUZZLES AT YOUR PACE
Explore 24 curated puzzles per language collection across four difficulty bands, from beginner to expert. Swipe to connect letters, tap individual tiles or type an answer. Use hints, reveal a letter or shuffle the wheel when you need a fresh perspective. English UK and English US editions share the same common-spelling collection.

SIX GARDEN ENVIRONMENTS
Progress from Sunrise Garden through Enchanted Forest, Blossom Valley, Crystal Lake and Golden Meadow to Moonlight Garden. First completions add blooms and earn hints. Progress and settings save on your device.

DAILY WORD PUZZLES, AVAILABLE OFFLINE
Return for a calendar-based puzzle selected from the included collection by local date. Daily play is free, with no streak penalties.

FREE TO START, ONE-TIME PREMIUM
The introductory four-puzzle chapter and daily play are free. An optional one-time Amazon purchase unlocks the remaining 20 puzzles and all six environments, plus an extra hint earned on each first completion. No subscription is required.

OPTIONAL VOICE AND ACCESSIBILITY
Choose larger text, high contrast, dark theme or reduced motion. Optional narration and push-to-talk commands depend on compatible installed device services. Speech recognition may need internet and send speech to its provider; WordBloom stores no microphone recordings. Touch and text gameplay remain available offline.

LANGUAGE AND CONTENT
Play in English UK/US, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Dutch, Polish or Portuguese Brazil/Portugal. Each language collection includes 24 puzzles. English editions share their collection and progress; other locale editions save progress separately. Portuguese editions share vocabulary. The curated collections accept their listed target and bonus words rather than every dictionary word. Uninstalling clears local progress; eligible premium purchases can be restored through Amazon.
