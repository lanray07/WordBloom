for WordBloom after being shown the declaration and its scope. The checkbox was
then checked in the open Review & Submit form. Amazon enabled **Submit App**.
That button was not clicked. The app and premium item remain unsubmitted and
unpublished.

selected Amazon marketplace, without additional licensing/clearance actions for
Amazon, including applicable encryption rules. The confirmation came from the
publisher; no legal classification or export-license determination is claimed
by the implementation tests.

This records completion of the checkbox action in the review form, rather than
an app submission. Amazon may require the certification to be checked again if
the page/session resets before a future submission.
