WordBloom 0.1.0 multilingual candidate. Touchscreen Fire tablet app; not intended for Fire TV/automotive. No WordBloom login or developer credentials are needed.

Start with Begin your journey. English puzzle 1 uses C, A, T, S: find CAT, ACT and SAT by swipe, tap then Submit word, or typed entry. The first four journey puzzles and daily play are free in every language. Daily puzzles come from the included collection according to local date. Included gameplay works offline.

Settings offers English UK/US, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Dutch, Polish and Portuguese Brazil/Portugal, with 24 curated puzzles per language collection. English editions share progress. Other locale editions save progress separately; Portuguese editions share vocabulary. Test accented letters and text entry after switching. Clearing app data or uninstalling resets local progress.

Optional voice depends on compatible installed speech services and microphone permission; recognition may send speech to its provider and need internet. Touch/text gameplay remains available without voice. Larger text, high contrast, reduced motion, dark theme and separate audio controls are available.

One-time premium entitlement: wordbloom.premium. Unlocks the remaining 20 journey puzzles in each available language, all six environments and expanded statistics; earns 4 hints rather than 3 on each first completion. No subscription or ads. Restore uses the purchasing Amazon account. Check price, cancellation, purchase and restore through Amazon Live App Testing; no real-money charge should be needed for the test account. Do not use the separate .dev simulated-purchase APK for production checks.

Physical KFTRWI Android 9 normal-debug checks passed: ten locale editions, large text/accented input, progress boundaries, offline play and touch gestures. English letter speech was confirmed clear. Amazon-delivered minified release checks also passed: live price, purchase cancellation, successful purchase, offline premium access and restore after clearing local app data.
