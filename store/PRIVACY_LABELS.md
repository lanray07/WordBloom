# Privacy label draft

The questionnaire is **saved for review**, not a publisher compliance
certification. Source and the speech/Amazon integrations informed these entries:

| Category | Collection | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| User IDs | Yes | Functionality: Amazon account-specific entitlement |
| Purchase history | Yes | Functionality: purchase, restore and cancellation |
| Voice or sound recordings | Yes | Optional installed speech recognition |

No advertising or analytics purpose was selected. Third-party transfer was not
selected for functional service-provider/user-initiated flows under
[Amazon's definitions](https://developer.amazon.com/docs/app-submission/appstore-privacy-labels.html#data-types).
The policy nevertheless explains speech-provider processing and Amazon
transactions. Payment-card data is entered outside WordBloom and is unavailable
to its code. Progress/preferences remain on the device.

Before submission, review actual SDK/service behavior on the distribution build
and update labels for any additional identifiable device data or diagnostics.
Provider behavior is not established by JVM tests. Support contact is voluntary
and outside the app; no email data or logs are uploaded automatically.
