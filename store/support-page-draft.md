# WordBloom support — contact details required

Swipe and release to submit a word, or tap tiles then Submit word. Undo removes the final tile; Clear resets selection. You can type answers. Only the puzzle's curated vocabulary is accepted.

First completions and new bonus words earn hints. Replays do not award extra hints. Progress stays on this device and reinstalling clears it.

If speech is unavailable, use touch/text. Your device may have no compatible recognition service. Speech may need internet; narration requires an installed TTS voice.

For premium access use Restore Amazon purchase while signed into the original Amazon account with a working Appstore connection. Wait for store confirmation. Never send passwords, payment information or audio recordings to support.

Publisher and support email/URL: to be supplied. Reports should include device model, OS version, app version and the visible error.
