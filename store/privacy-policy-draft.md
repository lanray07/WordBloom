# Privacy policy — publisher details required

WordBloom stores puzzle progress, hints, preferences and daily completion history on your device. It does not require a WordBloom account and includes no advertising or analytics service. Removing the app removes local progress. Android backups are disabled.

Microphone permission is optional and used only when you press Speak with an available recognition service. WordBloom stores no raw audio. Your device provider may process speech on its servers under its own terms and may require internet access. Declining permission leaves all puzzles playable through touch/text. TTS depends on the installed voice provider.

Amazon processes purchases under its terms. The app stores an app-specific Amazon user identifier and entitlement state privately on your device for offline access/restoration. It does not receive payment-card information. There is no WordBloom backend or progress upload.
