# WordBloom support

Contact the publisher, Lanray, at **banksmi@mail.com**. Include the app version,
Fire tablet model and a short description of the problem. Do not include payment
details, passwords or microphone recordings.

## Playing and progress

The included collection has 24 curated English puzzles. Swipe between letters,
tap letter tiles and submit, or type an answer. A tile instance cannot be used
twice in one word. Only that puzzle's target and bonus words are accepted.
Included puzzles and daily play work offline. Daily selection uses the device's
local date and the included collection. Progress saves on this device; clearing
app data or uninstalling resets it.

## Premium access

The first four puzzles and daily play are free. A one-time Amazon purchase
unlocks the full included collection. Use **Restore Amazon purchase** from the
premium screen while signed into the Amazon account used to buy. Purchasing
requires compatible Amazon store services and internet access. If a price is
unavailable, check your store installation and try again. Refunds and billing
questions are handled through Amazon's purchase support.

## Voice and accessibility

Voice features require a compatible speech or text-to-speech service on the
device. Recognition may require internet access and microphone permission.
Touch and text entry remain available. Settings include larger text, high
contrast, reduced motion and a dark theme.

## Languages and privacy

Gameplay currently supports English UK and English US, sharing the same
common-spelling collection. A translated store description does not mean the
puzzles have been translated. See [the privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).
