# WordBloom privacy policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026

WordBloom is published under the Amazon developer name **Lanray**. For support
or privacy questions, contact **banksmi@mail.com**.

## Data on your device

WordBloom saves puzzle progress, discovered words, hint balances, garden
milestones, daily completion history and preferences in the app's private storage
on your device. It has no WordBloom account, developer-operated backend,
advertising SDK or analytics SDK. Progress is not uploaded to a WordBloom server.
Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for the app.

You can remove this local data using your device's app-storage controls or by
uninstalling WordBloom. This resets progress and settings. It does not cancel an
Amazon purchase. Clearing app data also removes the local premium cache;
eligible purchases can be restored through Amazon.

## Optional microphone and voice services

Microphone permission is requested when you choose to use push-to-talk. You can
decline it and play through swiping, tapping or typing. WordBloom stops listening
when you leave the active app and does not save microphone recordings.

Speech recognition is provided by a compatible service installed on your device.
That service receives audio when you use push-to-talk and may send it to its own
servers. Recognition may require internet access. WordBloom receives the
recognized text to interpret a command or check a proposed word. Optional
text-to-speech sends words or prompts to the installed speech engine for spoken
playback. The service provider's privacy policy and device settings govern its
processing and retention. Check those before using voice features. WordBloom
does not guarantee offline speech recognition.

## Amazon purchases

Amazon's purchasing service processes the optional one-time premium purchase
and purchase restoration. WordBloom receives an app-specific Amazon user
identifier and purchase/entitlement information. It uses these to associate
premium access with the correct Amazon account, restore purchases and handle
cancellations. The app privately caches the identifier and entitlement state on
your device for offline access. It does not receive payment-card or bank details.

Amazon operates its own store and account services. See the
[Amazon privacy notice](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GX7NJQ4ZB8MHFRNJ)
for Amazon's processing, account controls and privacy choices. WordBloom cannot
delete Amazon's transaction records or a speech provider's records.

## Support communications

If you email support, your email provider and the support mailbox process the
address and information you choose to send so the publisher can respond. Do not
send passwords, payment-card details or microphone recordings. The app does not
automatically attach logs or progress data to email.

## Your choices and questions

Voice is optional. You can revoke microphone permission in device settings,
disable spoken guidance in WordBloom, delete local app data, or contact the
support address with a privacy question or request about information you have
sent to support. Requests concerning Amazon accounts, purchases or speech-service
records should also be directed to the relevant provider.

This policy describes the current WordBloom app. Changes to data handling will
be reflected here with an updated effective date.
